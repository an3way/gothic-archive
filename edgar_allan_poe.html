About Edgar Allan Poe

Edgar Allan Poe was an American writer and poet known for his macabre and Gothic tales, including The Tell-Tale Heart and The Fall of the House of Usher. His fiction and poetry find terror in obsession, grief, and the unsettling workings of the mind.
